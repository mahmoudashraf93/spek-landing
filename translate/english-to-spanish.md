## Translate English to Spanish online

Enter your English text in the left box, leave Spanish selected as the target, and press **Translate**. You can translate up to 500 characters per request. To translate Spanish to English, use the swap button and paste the Spanish message.

## Give the sentence some context

Spanish wording can depend on who is speaking and who is being addressed. Include a complete sentence, and check whether the result uses a level of formality that suits the conversation. Names, numbers, and times deserve a second look before you send a message.

This free tool offers Google's standard Spanish translation. It does not include a separate Mexican Spanish or Spain Spanish selector. For country-specific options in the iPhone app, read our [Mexican Spanish translator guide]({{ '/blog/mexican-spanish-translator-iphone/' | relative_url }}).

## Questions about the free Spanish translator

### Can I translate Spanish back to English?

Yes. Swap the languages to make Spanish the source and English the target. The same limits apply in both directions.

### Do I need to download Spek?

No. The web translator works in your browser with JavaScript and an internet connection. Spek for iPhone is a separate subscription app with voice, text, and photo translation.

### Can I translate a document or photo?

This web tool accepts short text. It does not upload documents, read photos, or listen to speech. You can paste a short passage you already have as text. Have a qualified person check consequential translations.
