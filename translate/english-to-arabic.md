## Translate English to Arabic

Type or paste an English sentence, choose Arabic as the target, and press **Translate**. Arabic output is displayed right to left. Copy the result to use it in a message. To understand Arabic text in English, swap the languages first.

You can submit up to 500 characters per translation. Arabic marks, spaces, punctuation, and emoji can count toward the allowance.

## Written Arabic and spoken dialects

This tool uses Google's standard Arabic translation. It does not offer a dedicated Egyptian, Levantine, or Gulf Arabic mode. Spoken Arabic varies by place and situation; a broadly understandable written translation may sound formal in an everyday conversation.

If your goal is a conversation with family or friends in Egypt, our [Egyptian Arabic translator guide]({{ '/blog/egyptian-arabic-translator-iphone/' | relative_url }}) explains the distinction and available iPhone options.

## Tips for clearer Arabic translations

Use complete sentences and make pronouns clear. Arabic wording can vary with the speaker and the person being addressed. Review mixed Arabic and English text, especially names, addresses, amounts, and dates. Automatic translation can misread dialect, informal spelling, or ambiguous phrases.

## Questions about the Arabic translator

### Can I translate Arabic to English here?

Yes. Use the swap control, paste Arabic into the source box, and translate into English. The source box also supports right-to-left text.

### Does it translate Arabizi or provide pronunciation?

There is no separate Arabizi conversion or pronunciation feature in this web tool. For Arabic input, use Arabic script where possible. The tool returns written translation only.

### Is it free without an account?

Yes. The free allowance is 10 translations per day, up to 500 characters each, subject to shared service limits. No account or app installation is needed.
