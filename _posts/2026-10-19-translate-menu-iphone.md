<h2 id="before-you-start">From a menu to a meal.</h2>

Exploring a new place is easier when you can read what is on the menu. Spek translates text in a photo on your iPhone and shows the result alongside the original.

Choose a clear menu photo, select the source and target languages, and tap Translate Image. The example here translates a French café menu into English.

For better results, keep the text in focus and well lit, and try the [clearer-results tips](#clearer-results).

<h2 class="visually-hidden" id="translate-a-menu">Translate a menu</h2>
<ol class="guide-steps">
  <li><h3>Choose a clear photo</h3><p>Open Spek’s Image tab. Choose Camera or Photo Library and select a clear photo. Use Change Image to pick another.</p></li>
  <li><h3>Set your languages</h3><p>Set From to the menu’s language and To to the language you want to read. Choose different languages. Explore Spek’s <a href="{{ '/#dialect-choices' | relative_url }}">country and dialect choices</a>.</p></li>
  <li><h3>Translate and read</h3><p>Tap Translate Image, then read the result alongside the original. Use Change Image to try another section.</p></li>
</ol>

<p class="article-fine">Internet connection required. Some features require an in-app purchase.</p>

<details class="guide-tips" id="clearer-results">
  <summary>If the text is hard to read</summary>
  <ul><li>Keep the menu flat, well lit, and free of glare or deep shadows.</li><li>Move closer to small text and keep the camera steady.</li><li>Try one section at a time instead of a whole page of tiny print.</li><li>Check the source and target languages, and retake a blurry photo.</li></ul>
  <p>A translation is an aid to understanding. Confirm unfamiliar ingredients with the restaurant, especially if you have an allergy or another dietary requirement.</p>
</details>
